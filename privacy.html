Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Pius Hermes Google Workspace is a private automation tool used only by its owner.

Google user data

The application accesses Google data only after the owner grants OAuth consent and only to perform owner-requested productivity functions such as email, calendar, task, contact, document, spreadsheet, and Drive operations.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Google user data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shared with third parties except when the owner explicitly requests an external action.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored credentials may be deleted or revoked by the owner at any time. Data retrieved for a task is retained only as needed for that task and the owner's records.
